GH Trust Loan Platform

Ready to launch — no wallet required

Product Owner Brief · August 2026

The GH Trust loan platform is built, tested, and ready for your team to start using. Customers can apply for loans. Your staff can review, approve, and disburse — all in one place. You do not need wallet or virtual account integration to go live with loans.

Bottom line: Loans work end-to-end today. Wallet funding is a separate feature we can add later. You can deploy now and start processing real applications immediately.

What this means for the business

  • Start earning from lending now — four loan products are ready (Business, Payday, Study, Asset).

  • Your team stays in control — configurable approval steps, document checks, and full audit trail on every application.
  • Customers get a smooth experience — apply on the app, upload documents, track status, and see active loans after disbursement.

Why wallet integration is not blocking you

How loans work today

  • Customer applies on the app
  • Staff approve internally
  • Money is sent to customer’s bank account
  • Loan is recorded in the system

What wallet would add (later)

  • In-app account balance
  • Fund wallet before fees
  • Pay installments in-app

Most MFIs already disburse via bank transfer and track repayments operationally. That is exactly how you can run from day one. Wallet integration becomes valuable when you want customers to pay entirely inside the app — but it is not a prerequisite for lending.

Repayment — what we capture now vs. what comes next

Product owners often ask about repayment plans. Here is a clear, practical answer:

At launch (Phase 1) — enough to operate

  • Each product defines interest rate, processing fee, and allowed repayment frequency
  • Customer selects how they want to repay when applying
  • Admin confirms the final approved amount and terms before disbursement
  • Operations continues tracking actual repayments using your existing tools (bank statements, Remita, spreadsheets) — same as today

Next release (Phase 2) — richer experience

  • Automatic installment schedule generated when a loan is disbursed
  • Customer sees upcoming due dates in the app
  • Admin sees full repayment plan per loan
  • Optional: automated collections (Remita, in-app payments)
Recommendation: Launch loans now with terms captured on every application. Add detailed repayment schedules and in-app collections as the next increment — not as a blocker to going live.

How repayment plans will be configured (when we add Phase 2)

We recommend keeping it simple for admins:

  • Per loan product — set interest rate, allowed loan duration options, and repayment frequency (e.g. monthly only for Study, salary date for Payday)
  • System generates the schedule — when a loan is disbursed, installments are calculated automatically
  • Exceptions only — credit team can adjust a single loan manually when needed, not as the default

This gives you flexibility without overwhelming staff with complex setup on day one.

Applications vs. active loans — a quick note

An application is what the customer submits. An active loan only appears after your team approves and disburses. This is normal — the admin portal shows applications in the queue; the loan book shows what has actually been funded.

How to go live — three simple steps

  1. Deploy the platform — Engineering hosts the backend, admin portal, and customer app in your staging or production environment (standard deployment — no wallet dependency).
  2. Configure your team — Create staff accounts, assign roles (Loan Officer, Credit, Branch Manager, Operations), and confirm loan product settings match your credit policy.
  3. Start processing — Customers apply on the app; your team reviews in the admin portal; disburse via bank transfer (automated or manual); loans appear in the book.

What to confirm before launch

  • Which loan products to enable on day one (all four, or a phased rollout?)
  • Final rates, fees, and document lists with credit team
  • Approval steps match how branches actually work
  • Disbursement: automated bank transfer or manual transfer + mark as disbursed in system
  • Repayment tracking: continue with current ops process until Phase 2 schedules
  • Staff training on admin portal (half-day walkthrough is sufficient)
